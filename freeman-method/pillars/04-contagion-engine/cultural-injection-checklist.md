**Tactical list for inserting your idea into ongoing trends and conversations**

## Pre-Launch Scan

Before any launch, scan these sources for injection opportunities:

- [ ] Trending topics on Twitter/X (last 48 hours)
- [ ] Trending on LinkedIn (industry-specific)
- [ ] Reddit front page (relevant subreddits)
- [ ] Google Trends (rising queries in your space)
- [ ] Industry news (last 7 days)
- [ ] Competitor announcements
- [ ] Cultural events (holidays, awards, launches)
- [ ] Influencer conversations in your space

## Injection Methods

| Method | When to use | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Newsjacking** | Breaking news in your space | "Here's what [event] means for [your category]" |
| **Trend riding** | Rising hashtag/topic | Use trending hashtag with your angle |
| **Contrarian take** | Popular opinion forming | "Everyone says X, but actually Y" |
| **Addition** | Existing conversation | Add valuable insight to trending thread |
| **Remix** | Popular format/meme | Adapt viral format to your message |
| **Partnership** | Complementary brand event | Co-create content around shared moment |

## Injection Rules

1. **Speed wins** — Inject within 24 hours of trend emergence
2. **Add value** — Don't just hijack; contribute to the conversation
3. **Stay on brand** — Injection must connect to your category narrative
4. **Prepare templates** — Have response frameworks ready before trends emerge
5. **Track results** — Log which injections drove the most reach
