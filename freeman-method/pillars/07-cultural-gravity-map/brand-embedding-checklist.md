# Brand Embedding Checklist™

**Step-by-step guide to integrate your brand into habits, communities, and daily lives**

---

## Level 1: Awareness (they know you exist)

- [ ] Brand appears in 3+ places they already visit daily
- [ ] Category name is used in conversations they overhear
- [ ] At least 1 influencer they trust has mentioned you
- [ ] Your content appears in their feed organically (not just ads)
- [ ] Someone they know has recommended you

---

## Level 2: Consideration (they think about you)

- [ ] Your category name is in their mental map of solutions
- [ ] They've visited your website or consumed your content
- [ ] They can explain what you do to someone else (using your terms)
- [ ] Your brand is associated with a specific identity they aspire to
- [ ] They've compared you to alternatives (using your frame)

---

## Level 3: Integration (you're part of their life)

- [ ] They use your category language in daily conversation
- [ ] Your product/service is part of their routine
- [ ] They identify as "a [your practitioner term]"
- [ ] They create content about you unprompted
- [ ] Your brand is part of how they define themselves

---

## Level 4: Advocacy (they spread you)

- [ ] They recommend you without being asked
- [ ] They defend your brand in conversations
- [ ] They create user-generated content featuring your brand
- [ ] They recruit others into your community
- [ ] They'd be upset if your brand disappeared
