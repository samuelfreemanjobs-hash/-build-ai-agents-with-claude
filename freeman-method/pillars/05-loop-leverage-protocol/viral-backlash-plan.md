## Response Protocol (when backlash hits)

### Hour 0-1: Assess
- [ ] Identify the emotion driving the backlash (anger, fear, mockery?)
- [ ] Determine if criticism has valid points
- [ ] Check if this is organic or competitor-driven
- [ ] Assess reach/velocity of the backlash

### Hour 1-4: Respond
- [ ] Deploy pre-planned response template (adapted to specifics)
- [ ] Acknowledge valid points (never dismiss legitimate criticism)
- [ ] Reframe toward your category narrative
- [ ] Do NOT get defensive or engage trolls

### Hour 4-24: Amplify
- [ ] Turn response into content (blog post, thread, video)
- [ ] Feed attention into growth loops (referrals, signups, community)
- [ ] Seed your lexicon in the response ("This is exactly why [your category term] matters")
- [ ] Monitor sentiment shift

### Day 2-7: Compound
- [ ] Publish "what we learned" content
- [ ] Update product/positioning if criticism was valid
- [ ] Track net reach gained from the backlash
- [ ] Document playbook for next time

## Rules of Antifragile Response

1. **Never delete** — Deleting amplifies the story
2. **Speed beats perfection** — Respond within hours, not days
3. **Acknowledge before reframing** — Validate before pivoting
4. **Own your frame** — Every response reinforces your category language
5. **Feed the loops** — Every backlash response should drive a growth loop
