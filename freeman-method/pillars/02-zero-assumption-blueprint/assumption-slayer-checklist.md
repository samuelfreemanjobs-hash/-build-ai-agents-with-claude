# Assumption Slayer Checklist™

**A diagnostic to kill inherited industry BS**

---

## Instructions

For each category below, list assumptions your industry believes. Mark each as:
- ✅ **Verified** — Evidence supports it
- ❌ **Debunked** — Evidence contradicts it
- ⚠️ **Outdated** — Was true, no longer is
- 🔄 **Reframe** — Partially true, but a better frame exists
