# White Space Heatmap™

**Color-coded market gap visualization for prioritization**

---

## How to Use

Rate each cell on two axes:
- **Demand intensity** (how badly do they want a solution?)
- **Competition density** (how crowded is the current supply?)

Plot opportunities in the grid. **Green = attack first. Red = avoid.**
