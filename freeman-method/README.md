## Key Metrics & Targets

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| HSR (Hook Share Rate) | % of audience sharing content/offers | ≥ 20% |
| OSM (Organic Spread Multiplier) | Organic reach ÷ paid reach | ≥ 2.5x |
| LRR (Loop Retention Rate) | % of loops active after 30 days | ≥ 80% |
| CTAR (Category Term Adoption Rate) | % of industry mentions using your terms | ≥ 60% |
| CTP (Cultural Touchpoint Penetration) | % of planned cultural integrations executed | ≥ 75% |
