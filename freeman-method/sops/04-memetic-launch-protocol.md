# Memetic Launch Protocol™ (MLP)

**Launch a product/idea so it spreads itself without heavy ad spend.**

---

## Protocol Overview

**Timeline:** 14 days pre-launch → launch day → 14 days post-launch

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Days -14 to -1)

### Days -14 to -10: Payload Design
- [ ] Complete [Meme Payload Builder™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/meme-payload-builder.md)
  - [ ] Winning phrase coined (5 people repeated it)
  - [ ] Primary emotion identified
  - [ ] Target identity defined
  - [ ] Visual anchor designed
  - [ ] Story arc written
- [ ] Score in [Talkability Scorecard™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/talkability-scorecard.csv) (target ≥ 50/70)

### Days -9 to -5: Spread Path Mapping
- [ ] Complete [Idea Virus Blueprint™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/idea-virus-blueprint.md)
  - [ ] Seed design finalized
  - [ ] Carrier 1 (early adopters) identified and briefed
  - [ ] Carrier 2 (network effect) planned
  - [ ] Carrier 3 (amplification) outreach started
- [ ] Map hooks in [Hook Forge™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/hook-forge.md)

### Days -4 to -1: Injection Prep
- [ ] Complete [Cultural Injection Checklist™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/cultural-injection-checklist.md) scan
- [ ] Pre-load 3 injection opportunities for launch week
- [ ] Prepare [PR Magnet Map™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/pr-magnet-map.md) pitches (send on launch day)
- [ ] Brief Carrier 1 advocates with share kit

---

## Phase 2: Launch Day

### Hour 0: Seed
- [ ] Publish core launch content (embeds full meme payload)
- [ ] Activate Carrier 1 (early adopters share simultaneously)
- [ ] Deploy first cultural injection
- [ ] Send PR pitches from PR Magnet Map™

### Hours 1-4: Monitor & Amplify
- [ ] Track share rate (target HSR ≥ 20%)
- [ ] Engage every comment and share
- [ ] Amplify best-performing variant
- [ ] Deploy second cultural injection if trend opportunity appears

### Hours 4-24: Compound
- [ ] Activate Carrier 2 (encourage network sharing)
- [ ] Publish follow-up content (different hook, same payload)
- [ ] Log all wins in [Momentum Multiplier Sheet™](../pillars/05-loop-leverage-protocol/momentum-multiplier-sheet.md)
- [ ] Feed attention into growth loops

---

## Phase 3: Post-Launch (Days +1 to +14)

### Days +1 to +3: Mutation Management
- [ ] Monitor how the meme is mutating across audiences
- [ ] Accept beneficial mutations, contain harmful ones
- [ ] Publish "what we're seeing" content (social proof of spread)

### Days +4 to +7: Amplification
- [ ] Activate Carrier 3 (influencer/media amplification)
- [ ] Deploy remaining cultural injections
- [ ] Turn early results into case study content

### Days +8 to +14: Loop Installation
- [ ] Install post-launch growth loops in [Loop Builder Canvas™](../pillars/05-loop-leverage-protocol/loop-builder-canvas.md)
- [ ] Begin [Narrative Domination Cycle™](02-narrative-domination-cycle.md)
- [ ] Measure OSM (target ≥ 2.5x organic vs paid reach)
