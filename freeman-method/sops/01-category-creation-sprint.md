# Category Creation Sprint™ (CCS)

**Turn raw research into a fully named, positioned, and mapped-out category in 7 days or less.**

---

## Sprint Overview

| Day | Focus | Tools | Output |
|-----|-------|-------|--------|
| Day 1 | Discovery | Category Gap Canvas™, Opportunity Radar™ | White space identified |
| Day 2 | Deconstruction | Zero Assumption Blueprint™, Assumption Slayer™ | Assumptions killed |
| Day 3 | Naming | Market Lexicon Playbook™, Language Lock™ | Category named |
| Day 4 | Narrative | Remarkability Trigger Matrix™, Hook Forge™ | Story crafted |
| Day 5 | Validation | Talkability Scorecard™, Non-Customer Map™ | Concept validated |
| Day 6 | Assets | PR Magnet Map™, Meme Payload Builder™ | Launch assets ready |
| Day 7 | Launch prep | All tools reviewed | Category launch-ready |

---

## Day 1: Discovery

### Morning
- [ ] Complete [Category Gap Canvas™](../pillars/01-category-gap-canvas/category-gap-canvas.md)
- [ ] Score opportunities in [Opportunity Radar™](../pillars/01-category-gap-canvas/opportunity-radar.csv)
- [ ] Map non-customers in [Non-Customer Map™](../pillars/01-category-gap-canvas/non-customer-map.md)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Plot opportunities on [White Space Heatmap™](../pillars/01-category-gap-canvas/white-space-heatmap.md)
- [ ] Select top 1-3 attack targets
- [ ] Document category hypothesis

**Day 1 Output:** Category hypothesis with scored white space

---

## Day 2: Deconstruction

### Morning
- [ ] Complete [Zero Assumption Blueprint™](../pillars/02-zero-assumption-blueprint/zero-assumption-blueprint.md)
- [ ] Run [Assumption Slayer Checklist™](../pillars/02-zero-assumption-blueprint/assumption-slayer-checklist.md)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Rebuild offer in [Market Rebuild Canvas™](../pillars/02-zero-assumption-blueprint/market-rebuild-canvas.md)
- [ ] Score ideas in [Feasibility-to-Momentum Model™](../pillars/02-zero-assumption-blueprint/feasibility-to-momentum.csv)

**Day 2 Output:** Rebuilt offer from first principles (≥ 50% assumptions killed)

---

## Day 3: Naming

### Morning
- [ ] Define category language in [Market Lexicon Playbook™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/market-lexicon-playbook.md)
- [ ] Coin terms in [Language Lock™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/language-lock.md)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Build language hierarchy
- [ ] Test category name with 5 target customers
- [ ] Finalize proprietary terminology (minimum 5 terms)

**Day 3 Output:** Named category with proprietary lexicon

---

## Day 4: Narrative

### Morning
- [ ] Complete [Remarkability Trigger Matrix™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/remarkability-trigger-matrix.md)
- [ ] Forge hooks in [Hook Forge™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/hook-forge.md)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Craft category narrative (before/after/trigger story)
- [ ] Score talkability in [Talkability Scorecard™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/talkability-scorecard.csv)
- [ ] Target: ≥ 50/70 talkability score

**Day 4 Output:** Category narrative with scored hooks

---

## Day 5: Validation

### Morning
- [ ] Interview 5 non-customers from Non-Customer Map™
- [ ] Test category name and language with each
- [ ] Record: Did they repeat your terms? Did they express the pain?

### Afternoon
- [ ] Refine based on feedback
- [ ] Update category hypothesis
- [ ] Confirm: pain ≥ 8/10, name is memorable, no competitor owns language

**Day 5 Output:** Validated category with customer evidence

---

## Day 6: Assets

### Morning
- [ ] Build PR angles in [PR Magnet Map™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/pr-magnet-map.md)
- [ ] Design meme payload in [Meme Payload Builder™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/meme-payload-builder.md)

### Afternoon
- [ ] Create launch content kit (3 social posts, 1 blog outline, 1 email)
- [ ] Map spread path in [Idea Virus Blueprint™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/idea-virus-blueprint.md)

**Day 6 Output:** Launch-ready content and spread plan

---

## Day 7: Launch Prep

### Morning
- [ ] Review all sprint outputs
- [ ] Complete sprint checklist (below)
- [ ] Schedule launch date

### Afternoon
- [ ] Brief team on category language
- [ ] Set up [Terminology Dominance Tracker™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/terminology-dominance-tracker.csv)
- [ ] Plan Week 1 post-launch actions

**Day 7 Output:** Launch-ready category

---

## Sprint Completion Checklist

- [ ] White space identified and scored
- [ ] ≥ 50% industry assumptions killed
- [ ] Category named with proprietary lexicon (≥ 5 terms)
- [ ] Talkability score ≥ 50/70
- [ ] 5 customer validations completed
- [ ] Launch content kit ready
- [ ] Spread path mapped
- [ ] Tracking systems in place
