# Narrative Domination Cycle™ (NDC)

**Ensure your story stays top-of-mind and uncontested in the market.**

---

## Cycle Overview

**Frequency:** Weekly execution, monthly review

| Week | Focus | Tools |
|------|-------|-------|
| Week 1 | Scan & seed | Cultural Injection Checklist™, Lexicon Expansion Map™ |
| Week 2 | Create & spread | Contagion Engine™, Meme Payload Builder™ |
| Week 3 | Amplify & measure | Terminology Dominance Tracker™, PR Magnet Map™ |
| Week 4 | Review & adjust | All pillar metrics, Master Ops Board™ |

---

## Weekly Routine

### Monday: Trend Scan
- [ ] Run [Cultural Injection Checklist™](../pillars/04-contagion-engine/cultural-injection-checklist.md) pre-launch scan
- [ ] Identify 2-3 injection opportunities for the week
- [ ] Check [Terminology Dominance Tracker™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/terminology-dominance-tracker.csv) for competitor language shifts

### Tuesday-Wednesday: Content & Spread
- [ ] Deploy 1 cultural injection (from Monday scan)
- [ ] Publish 1 content piece embedding category language
- [ ] Seed 1 proprietary term in a new channel ([Lexicon Expansion Map™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/lexicon-expansion-map.md))

### Thursday: Amplification
- [ ] Amplify best-performing content from the week
- [ ] Engage with anyone using your category language (thank, share, connect)
- [ ] Pitch 1 PR angle from [PR Magnet Map™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/pr-magnet-map.md)

### Friday: Measure & Plan
- [ ] Update [Terminology Dominance Tracker™](../pillars/06-market-lexicon-playbook/terminology-dominance-tracker.csv)
- [ ] Log wins in [Momentum Multiplier Sheet™](../pillars/05-loop-leverage-protocol/momentum-multiplier-sheet.md)
- [ ] Plan next week's narrative focus

## Narrative Health Check

| Signal | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Competitors using your terms | 2+ competitors | 1 competitor | 0 competitors |
| Media mentions | Weekly | Monthly | None in 30 days |
| Customer language | They use your terms | Mixed language | They use competitor terms |
| Search volume for category name | Growing | Flat | Declining |

---

## Escalation Protocol

**If narrative is weakening (Warning or Critical):**

1. Run emergency [Hook Forge™](../pillars/03-remarkability-trigger-matrix/hook-forge.md) session
2. Deploy [Viral Backlash Plan™](../pillars/05-loop-leverage-protocol/viral-backlash-plan.md) if criticism is involved
3. Increase cultural injection frequency to daily for 1 week
4. Pitch 3 PR angles in 48 hours
5. Re-run [Category Gap Canvas™](../pillars/01-category-gap-canvas/category-gap-canvas.md) to find fresh angles
