## The Strategic Bet (One Paragraph)

The marketing industry sells tactics (ads, funnels, SEO). Nobody sells **market environment engineering** — the discipline of naming categories, owning narrative, and building antifragile growth systems. The Freeman Method™ owns that category. Competitors will be forced to say "category design" or "narrative strategy" — but we coined **Market Environment Engineering** and **NDCC**. The certification model turns practitioners into carriers of our lexicon. Every certified consultant seeds our language into client markets. The business compounds: certify → consultants deploy → client wins → case studies → more certification demand.
