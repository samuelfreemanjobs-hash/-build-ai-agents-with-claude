# HUNTER Intelligence — Business Model

## One-Liner
AI-powered operational intelligence for mid-market manufacturers — we find friction, score opportunities, and deliver systems that eliminate it.

## Positioning
**Category:** Operational Intelligence / Manufacturing Systems Integration
**Not:** Generic consulting, ERP implementation, or IT support
**Is:** Fast, focused, outcome-driven systems that replace spreadsheets and manual handoffs in 2-4 weeks

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Industry | CNC, Automotive Tier II, 3PL, Tool & Die, Wire Harness, Fabrication |
| Size | 50–500 employees |
| Revenue | $10M–$100M annual |
| Location | US Midwest, Southeast, Tennessee corridor |
| Decision Maker | VP Operations, Plant Manager, COO, Owner |
| Pain | Spreadsheet chaos, no real-time KPIs, manual QA data entry, disconnected systems |
| Budget | $15K–$40K project budget available |
| Trigger | New OEM contract, quality audit failure, leadership change, competitor modernization |

## Revenue Model

### Primary: Project-Based (80% of revenue)
7 packaged services at $15K–$35K, 2-4 week delivery

### Secondary: Retainer (20% of revenue, growing)
$4,500/mo ongoing optimization after project delivery

### Lead Magnet: Free Diagnostic
15-min call + custom operational friction report (give-before-ask)

## Competitive Moat

1. **AI Scoring Engine** — Only firm that scores manufacturing leads on 7 dimensions before first contact
2. **Give-Before-Ask** — Free diagnostic reports that demonstrate expertise before asking for money
3. **Speed** — 2-4 week delivery vs. 3-6 month consulting engagements
4. **Vertical Focus** — Deep manufacturing expertise, not generalist IT
5. **Autonomous Pipeline** — n8n + HUNTER OS runs discovery, scoring, outreach 24/7

## Go-To-Market Motion

```
DISCOVER (n8n + Apify) → SCORE (Gemini) → OUTREACH (Resend) → DIAGNOSTIC (free) → PROPOSAL (AI) → CLOSE → DELIVER → RETAIN
```

**Your time per deal:** ~8 hours total (discovery call, proposal review, kickoff, delivery oversight)
**System time per deal:** ~40 hours automated (scoring, outreach, follow-up, reporting)
