# Freeman Intel — ICP & GTM Playbook

Metro Detroit automotive, logistics, and industrial manufacturing. **Plant inbound** wedge.

---

## Buyer personas

### Primary: Inbound Logistics Manager

- Owns appointment scheduling, carrier coordination, ASN quality
- Accountable for OTIF and chargebacks from receiving errors
- Reports to plant manager or materials director
- **Quote:** “My job is everything that happens before the truck hits the dock.”

### Primary: Receiving Supervisor

- Runs dock doors and receiving clerks
- First to feel unscheduled trucks and bad ASNs
- **Quote:** “Half my day is ‘do we have paperwork for this load?’”

### Secondary: Plant Scheduler / Materials Traffic Coordinator

- Bridges production need dates with inbound timing
- Escalates hot parts
- **Quote:** “I need to know if today’s inbound is lying to me.”

### Secondary: 3PL Inbound Desk Lead

- Manages inbound for multiple plants from one desk
- Wants repeatable rules per plant profile
- **Quote:** “Every plant has a different routing guide nightmare.”

| Stack | Email + PDF + spreadsheets | Fully integrated WMS with no email |

| Size | 20–500 employees | Mega-OEM corporate inbound |
| Geography | SE Michigan + Toledo corridor | National broker shop |

## Discovery questions (first call)

1. What % of trucks arrive **without** a matching ASN or appointment?  
2. Where does the **dock schedule** live today?  
3. What happens when **ASN ≠ PO line**? Who gets involved?  
4. Top **three email types** per day (carrier ETA, supplier ASN, internal hot part)?  
5. Which **portal** do you open every day (if any)?  
6. One recent **line slow or line down** tied to inbound—what happened?  
7. How do you export data to receiving today (copy/paste, sheet, WMS screen)?

## Pilot structure (60 days)

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 1 | Connect email; upload sample expected inbound + routing rules |
| 2 | Tune extraction on 20 real ASN emails |
| 3 | Run parallel: Freeman Intel queue vs manual desk |
| 4–8 | Daily exception review; measure pre-gate validation % |
| 9 | Readout: metrics + case study draft |
| 10 | Convert or extend with Dock & ETA preview |

**Pilot success definition:** ≥80% of sampled inbound validated or flagged **before** carrier arrival notification.
