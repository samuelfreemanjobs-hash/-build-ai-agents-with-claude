# Freeman Intel

**AI inbound logistics desk for Metro Detroit automotive and industrial plants.**

Freeman Intel turns supplier ASN emails, carrier updates, and expected inbound spreadsheets into validated dock-ready shipments—with human approval on everything that affects the gate.

## Who it's for

- Tier 2 / Tier 3 automotive supplier plants
- Industrial manufacturers (steel, tooling, machining) with strict receiving rules
- Small 3PL inbound desks serving plants in the Detroit–Toledo corridor

## What we sell (customer bundles)

| Bundle | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| **Inbound Readiness Team** | ASN intake, routing compliance, mismatch flags, supplier chase |
| **Dock & ETA Team** | Dock schedule drafts, carrier ETA watch, hot-part escalation |
| **Receipt & Dispute Team** | Lot/cert chase, receipt discrepancies, chargeback defense packs |
