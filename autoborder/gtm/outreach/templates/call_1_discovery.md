# Call 1 — SDR Discovery Script (30 minutes)
**Prospect:** {contact_name}, {contact_title} — {company_name} ({city})

---

## Opening (2 min)

"{contact_name}, thanks for the time. I'll keep this to 30 minutes.

We help automotive suppliers in {city} automate USMCA RVC calculations from SAP/Oracle BOM data — CBP-compliant, audit-ready, deployed in 72 hours.

Before I show anything — I want to understand your current process."

---

## Discovery Questions (20 min)

### Customs & Compliance
1. "How many customs brokers do you use for US-bound shipments?"
2. "When did you last recalculate RVC on your top complex parts?"
3. "Have you been through a CBP Rapid Response audit in the last 18 months?" *(listen for pain)*
4. "Who owns RVC calculation internally — supply chain, finance, or a consultant?"

### RVC Method
5. "Are you using the **roll-up** or **build-down** method for RVC?"
6. "How many active BOMs do you need USMCA certification on?"
7. "What's your current error rate on RVC recalculations?" *(benchmark: industry ~40% in Excel)*

### Quantify Pain
8. "Roughly what's your quarterly import value on USMCA-eligible parts?" *(target: {quarterly_import_value})*
9. "If RVC math is wrong, what's the exposure — duty repayment, penalties, or lost preferential treatment?"

---

## Transition to Call 2 (5 min)

"{contact_name}, based on what you've shared — especially around {part_focus} — I think there's a concrete number here.

For Call 2, I'd like to bring our Solution Architect. They'll screen-share a live RVC graph of a part similar to yours and show exactly where non-originating content is hitting your score.

Can we schedule 60 minutes next week? I'll need one part number from your SAP system — or we can use our sandbox demo."
