## 1. Purpose

Company wishes to participate in the AutoBorder Comply Design Partner Program ("Program") to evaluate CBP-compliant USMCA Regional Value Content (RVC) calculations against Company's internal methods.

## 2. Design Partner Trial

AutoBorder agrees to provide, at no cost to Company:

(a) Mapping and RVC calculation of Company's **top 100 most complex parts** within **72 hours** of SAP/Oracle read-only connector deployment;

(b) Forensic PDF audit documentation for each mapped part;

(c) Side-by-side accuracy comparison against Company's internal RVC calculations.
