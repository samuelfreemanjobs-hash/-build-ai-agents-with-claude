CATEGORY 1: Research & Assets Creation (4 Prompts)

1. The "Deep Dive" Avatar Research Use this before you write a single word of copy. It reveals what your customers actually want. "Act as an expert market researcher. I am selling [PRODUCT NAME] to [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Please generate a psychographic profile of my ideal customer. I need to know: The '3 AM' Worries: What keeps them awake at night regarding [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]? The 'Enemy': Who or what do they secretly blame for their failure? The 'Dream': If money/time were no object, what would their life look like after using my product? Output this as a structured table."

2. The "Grand Slam" Offer Creator Use this to turn a boring product into an irresistible deal. "Act as Alex Hormozi. I have a core product called [PRODUCT NAME] priced at [PRICE]. Help me turn this into a 'Grand Slam Offer' by adding value without adding cost. Please suggest: 3 Scarcity Tactics: (e.g., limited seats, time deadlines). 3 Urgency Triggers: (Why they must act now). 3 Bonuses: Create 3 digital bonuses (checklists, templates, guides) that solve specific implementation problems for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Guarantee: Write a 'risk-reversal' guarantee that removes all fear of buying."

3. The "Chameleon" Copywriter (Tone Shifter) Use this to rewrite boring content into exciting copy. "I am pasting a piece of marketing text below. I want you to rewrite it in the style of [FAMOUS EXPERT OR STYLE] (e.g., David Ogilvy, witty & sarcastic, or professional & corporate). Goals for the rewrite: Make it punchier. Use short sentences. Focus on the benefit: [MAIN BENEFIT]. Original Text: [PASTE YOUR TEXT HERE]"

4. The "Midjourney" Visual Prompter Use this to generate image descriptions you can feed into AI art tools for ads. "Act as a professional art director. I need an image for a Facebook Ad selling [PRODUCT NAME]. Please write 3 detailed image prompts I can put into Midjourney/DALL-E. Vibe: [DESIRED EMOTION: e.g., Energetic, Trustworthy, Luxurious]. Subject: A happy user achieving [DESIRED RESULT]. Lighting/Style: Cinematic lighting, photorealistic, 4k. Aspect Ratio: 1:1."

CATEGORY 2: Content Strategy & Ideation (5 Prompts)

5. The "Content Pillar" Architect "I need to build 4 content pillars for my personal brand in the [NICHE] space. My target audience is [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Please suggest 4 distinct content pillars (themes) and list 5 video topic ideas under each pillar that would appeal to beginners."

6. The "Viral Hook" Generator "I am creating a short-form video about [TOPIC]. Please generate 10 viral hooks (opening sentences) that utilize 'Pattern Interrupts' and 'Curiosity Gaps' to stop the scroll immediately. Keep them under 5 seconds spoken length."

7. The "Complex-to-Simple" Analogy Finder "I am trying to explain the concept of [COMPLEX TOPIC] to a complete beginner. Please provide 3 metaphors or analogies from everyday life (like cooking, driving, or sports) that make this concept instantly understandable."

8. The "Trend Jacking" Expert "A current trending topic is [CURRENT NEWS/TREND]. How can I relate this trend to my industry of [YOUR INDUSTRY] in a way that is relevant, educational, and not cringey? Give me 3 content angles."

9. The "Objection Killer" FAQ "List the top 10 objections someone might have before buying [PRODUCT NAME]. Then, for each objection, write a short social media post caption that addresses and neutralizes that objection without sounding salesy."

10. The "Content Multiplier" (Repurposing Engine) Turn one asset into a week's worth of traffic. "I have a long-form video/blog post about [TOPIC]. Please repurpose this into a 'Content Waterfall': 3 Twitter/X Threads: Summarizing the key points. 5 LinkedIn Text Posts: Each focusing on one specific sub-point. 5 Short-Form Video Scripts: (TikTok/Reels) focusing on the 'Hook' and 'Result'. 1 Newsletter Email: Teasing the full content."

11. The "Netflix-Style" Series Planner Create binge-worthy content that keeps people coming back. "I want to create a 5-part educational content series on social media about [TOPIC]. Goal: Structure it like a Netflix mini-series. Part 1: The Pilot (The big problem/mystery). Part 2-4: The Journey (Steps, mistakes, and small wins). Part 5: The Season Finale (The final result and a Call to Action). Write the titles and a 1-sentence synopsis for each part."

12. The "Blue Ocean" Angle Finder Say what nobody else is saying. "Everyone in the [YOUR NICHE] industry is talking about [COMMON TOPIC] in the same way (usually saying [COMMON ADVICE]). Please give me 3 'Contrarian' or 'Blue Ocean' angles that challenge this common advice. I want to stand out by offering a fresh, unique perspective."

13. The "Reddit Detective" (Market Pulse) Find the hidden questions your customers are asking. "Act as a researcher. I want you to simulate a search on Reddit (subreddits: [LIST SUBREDDITS]) regarding the keyword [KEYWORD]. List 10 specific questions, complaints, or struggles that real users are discussing right now. I will use these as topics for my next YouTube videos."

14. The "Data-to-Drama" Storyteller Turn boring numbers into exciting narratives. "I have a statistic that says: [INSERT STATISTIC, e.g., 80% of businesses fail]. Please turn this dry stat into a compelling story or social media post. Hook: Start with the shocking number. Body: Visualize what that number actually looks like in real life (humanize the data). Lesson: Explain how to be part of the successful percentage using my method."

Please suggest 3 distinct color palettes (Primary, Secondary, and Accent colors) with their Hex Codes and explain why each palette works for this specific audience."

16. The "Font Pairing" Expert "I need a typography pairing for my website. My brand personality is [BRAND PERSONALITY, e.g., MODERN, CLASSIC, PLAYFUL]. Suggest 3 font combinations (one for Headings, one for Body Text). Ensure they are Google Fonts compatible and legible on mobile. Explain the 'vibe' of each pairing."

17. The "High-Converting" Landing Page Layout "Describe the visual layout for a high-converting landing page selling a [PRICE] product. Above the Fold: What image/video placement works best? Visual Hierarchy: How should I use whitespace and contrast to guide the eye to the CTA? Section Flow: Describe the visual order of sections from top to bottom."

18. The "YouTube Thumbnail" Director "I am making a video titled [VIDEO TITLE]. Describe 3 click-worthy thumbnail concepts. Concept 1: Focus on emotion (face). Concept 2: Focus on the 'Before & After' result. Concept 3: Focus on a curiosity-inducing object. For each, describe the background color, text overlay, and central image composition."

19. The "Ad Creative" visualizer (Meta Ads) "I need 5 visual concepts for a Facebook Ad image selling [PRODUCT]. Instead of generic stock photos, describe 5 'Pattern Interrupt' visuals that would stop a user from scrolling. Format: 1:1 Square. Text Overlay: Keep it under 20% of the image."

20. The "Infographic" Structurer "I have a blog post with these 5 key data points: [LIST DATA POINTS]. Describe how to turn this into a viral infographic. Visual Metaphor: What is the central theme (e.g., a roadmap, a pyramid, an iceberg)? Flow: How should the user read it? Icons: Suggest icons for each data point."

21. The "Slide Deck" Aesthetics "I am creating a pitch deck for [INVESTORS/CLIENTS]. My current slides look boring. Suggest a visual theme based on [BRAND STYLE]. Backgrounds: Solid, gradient, or image-based? Image Style: Cutouts, black & white, or illustrations? Data Visualization: How should I style my charts to look modern?"

22. The "UI/UX" Audit "Act as a UX designer. I am designing a mobile checkout page. List 5 'Dark Patterns' I must avoid to build trust, and 5 'Trust Elements' (visual cues) I should add near the 'Buy Button' to increase conversion rates."

23. The "Social Grid" Planner "I want my Instagram grid to look cohesive and aesthetic. My niche is [NICHE]. Suggest a 'Checkerboard' or 'Row-by-Row' visual strategy. Post Type A: (e.g., Quote card with dark background). Post Type B: (e.g., Lifestyle photo with light filter). Explain how to alternate them for the best visual impact."

24. The "Logo" Brainstormer "I need ideas for a logo for [COMPANY NAME]. We do [SERVICE]. Suggest 5 concepts: Minimalist (Wordmark only). Abstract Icon (Geometric shape). Literal Icon (Related to the industry). Emblem (Vintage style). Monogram (Initials). Describe the visual elements for each."

CATEGORY 4: Campaign Ideas (10 Prompts)

25. The "Seasonal" Campaign Planner "Plan a 3-phase marketing campaign for [UPCOMING HOLIDAY, e.g., DIWALI/BLACK FRIDAY]. Phase 1 (The Tease): 7 days before. What content goes out? Phase 2 (The Launch): The Big Offer details. Phase 3 (The Close): The final 48 hours scarcity push. The product is [PRODUCT NAME]."

26. The "Gamification" Strategist "I want to run a contest to generate leads for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Design a 'Gamified' campaign where users earn points for actions (e.g., sharing, commenting). The Hook: What is the grand prize? The Mechanics: How do they enter? The Viral Loop: How do they get extra entries by referring friends?"

27. The "Challenge" Funnel Architect "Create a concept for a '5-Day Live Challenge' to sell my high-ticket coaching program [PROGRAM NAME]. Title of Challenge: Needs to promise a specific result in 5 days. Day 1-4 Content: What small wins do they get each day? Day 5: The Masterclass and the Pitch. Goal: Build trust before selling."

28. The "Guerrilla Marketing" Stunt "I have a budget of [BUDGET]. Give me 3 unconventional, 'Guerrilla Marketing' ideas to get attention for my local business [BUSINESS TYPE]. These should be low-cost but high-impact stunts that people would want to take photos of and share on social media."

29. The "User-Generated Content" (UGC) Wave "I want my customers to create content for me. Design a campaign that incentivizes users to post photos of themselves using [PRODUCT]. Campaign Hashtag: Catchy and unique. Incentive: What do they get? (Discount, feature, freebie?) Theme: What specifically should they take a photo of?"

30. The "Flash Sale" (Inventory Clear-out) "I need to clear out old inventory of [PRODUCT]. Plan a 24-hour 'Vault' sale. Angle: 'We found a box of unreleased...' or 'Spring Cleaning'. The Offer: Bundle pricing ideas. Email Sequence: 3 emails (Morning, Afternoon, 1 Hour Left)."

31. The "Collaboration" Cross-Promo "I want to partner with a non-competing brand in the [RELATED INDUSTRY] space. Suggest 3 campaign ideas where we can cross-pollinate our audiences. Idea 1: Joint Webinar. Idea 2: Co-created Bundle. Idea 3: Social Media Takeover. Explain the benefit for both parties."

32. The "Birthday" Bash "It is our company's 5th anniversary. Plan a 'Birthday Week' campaign. Day 1-4: Giveaways and gratitude posts (no selling). Day 5: The 'Big Birthday Offer' (our biggest discount of the year). Goal: Brand loyalty and sales."

33. The "Lost Customer" Win-Back "Design a campaign specifically for people who canceled their subscription 3+ months ago. The 'Olive Branch': Acknowledging we miss them. The 'What's New': Highlight feature updates they missed. The 'Come Back' Offer: A special rate for the first 3 months if they return this week."

34. The "Newsjacking" Pivot "There is currently a lot of news attention on [CURRENT EVENT/TREND]. How can I ethically create a campaign for [PRODUCT] that ties into this conversation? Angle: How does my product help navigate this new situation? Tone: Helpful, not opportunistic. Content: Blog post and Email blast ideas."
