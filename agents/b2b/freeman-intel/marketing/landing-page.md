## How it works

### Section title

Email in. Validated outbound. You approve.

### Steps

1. **Connect** Gmail or Outlook (+ upload expected inbound CSV/Sheet)  
2. **Extract** ASN and carrier emails into structured inbound records  
3. **Validate** against your plant rules and expected lines  
4. **Review** side-by-side source vs extracted fields with pass/warn/fail  
5. **Approve** → export to dock schedule, clipboard, or draft chase email  

### Diagram (for design)

```
Inbox + PDF  →  Freeman Intel  →  Approval queue  →  Dock schedule / comms
     ↑              ↑                    ↑
Expected inbound   Plant rules      Human always
(CSV/Sheet)        (routing guide)  for gate impact
```

## FAQ

**Do we need a TMS or WMS integration?**  
No. Freeman Intel starts with email, PDF, and spreadsheet export. Integrations come when your team is ready.

**Will AI auto-assign dock doors or book carriers?**  
Not without your approval in v1. We draft and flag; humans approve gate-impacting actions.

**Who is this for?**  
Inbound logistics and receiving teams at automotive supplier and industrial manufacturing plants in Metro Detroit and the Toledo corridor.

**What counts as an inbound event?**  
One processed ASN email, carrier update, or matched inbound record run through validation.

**How fast can we pilot?**  
Typically two weeks to first validated queue if you can share sample ASNs and expected inbound format.

**Can a 3PL use this for multiple plants?**  
Yes. Plant Plus supports multiple plant profiles with separate rules per site.
