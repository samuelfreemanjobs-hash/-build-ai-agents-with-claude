# Freeman Intel — Product Portfolio

Freeman Intel is the umbrella for logistics AI products targeting **Metro Detroit automotive and industrial** markets.

## Products

| Product | Buyer | Wedge | Status |
|---------|-------|-------|--------|
| **[Plant Inbound](../README.md)** (Freeman Intel core) | Inbound logistics manager, receiving supervisor | ASN + gate readiness before truck arrives | Plan complete |
| **[AI Proposals Agent™](../../ai-proposals-agent/)** | BD, sales, solutions | Logistics RFP proposals in ~75 min, every number traces | Design + console v2 |

## Go-to-market pairing

Logistics providers in the corridor often need **both**:

- **Win** the contract → AI Proposals Agent™  
- **Operate** the inbound → Freeman Intel Plant Inbound  

Bundle pitch: *"Win Detroit auto lanes and run the dock without chargebacks."*

## Shared principles

1. Human approval on high-impact actions  
2. Compliance-first (OTIF, ISO, routing rules)  
3. Email / document-first — no TMS API required to start  
4. Outcome teams, not agent marketplaces
