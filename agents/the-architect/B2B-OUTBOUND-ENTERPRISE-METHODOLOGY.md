## B2B Cold Outbound Pipeline Generator

### Multi-channel stack

| Channel | Asset | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold email** | 3–7 touch sequence | 50–125 words | Pattern interrupt + peer proof + one CTA |
| **LinkedIn** | Connection note + InMail + comment strategy | <300 chars note | Warm context before pitch |
| **Phone** | Voicemail + live opener | 20–30 sec VM | Callback trigger |
| **Direct mail** | Dimensional or letter | 1 page | Pattern break for C-suite |
| **Video** | Loom/Vidyard 1:1 | 60–90 sec | "Made this for [Company]" |

### Cold email architecture (per touch)

```
Touch 1: Relevant insight (not pitch) + soft CTA (reply/question)
Touch 2: Case study — similar company + metric
Touch 3: Mechanism tease — "how [peer] fixed [problem]"
Touch 4: Objection pre-handle (timing, budget, incumbent)
Touch 5: Breakup / value bomb — best asset, door open
```

**Rules:** No "Hope this finds you well." One idea per email. Personalization = their words (10-K, LinkedIn post, job posting), not `{FirstName}` only.

### Stakeholder messaging matrix

| Role | Opener frame | Proof type | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gatekeeper** | Respect time; clear ask | Brief, professional | Route to champion |
| **Champion** | Make them hero internally | Peer case study | 15-min discovery |
| **Decision Maker** | Strategic outcome + risk | ROI model, board-ready | Executive briefing |
| **Procurement** | TCO, terms, compliance | Comparison matrix, MSA-ready | RFP response / pricing call |
| **C-Suite** | Market shift + cost of inaction | Analyst-style brevity | Strategic conversation |

## ABM Personalization Matrix (1:1 Enterprise)

For named accounts — generate **account-specific** assets:

| Asset | Personalization inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing page** | Company name, industry pain, competitor mention, logo bar with peers | Custom LP copy blocks |
| **Pitch deck** | Their initiatives (earnings call, press), mapped to your mechanism | 8–12 slide narrative |
| **1:1 video script** | "[Name], I noticed [specific trigger]…" | 60–90 sec Loom script |
| **Executive brief** | 1-page ROI for their stack | PDF copy + chart spec |
| **Email to champion** | Forwardable internal sell | "Share this with [CFO]" asset |

### ABM personalization data sources

- 10-K / annual report (public cos)
- LinkedIn job postings (pain signals)
- Tech stack (BuiltWith, job reqs)
- Recent news / funding / leadership change
- Competitor wins/losses in segment

**Template fields:** `{{account_name}}`, `{{trigger_event}}`, `{{industry_pain}}`, `{{peer_proof}}`

## Proposal & RFP Win Architecture

### High-ticket proposal structure

```
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY    — outcome in their words (1 page max)
2. SITUATION            — their problem, quantified
3. APPROACH             — your mechanism (named system)
4. SCOPE & DELIVERABLES — MECE, no ambiguity
5. TIMELINE & MILESTONES — phased value delivery
6. TEAM & CREDENTIALS   — proof pyramid layer 5
7. INVESTMENT           — anchor high, options tiered
8. TERMS                — payment, IP, cancellation
9. NEXT STEPS           — single clear action
10. APPENDIX            — case studies, SOW detail, security
```

### Procurement resistance killers

| Objection | Proposal move |
|---|---|
| **Price** | ROI model + cost of inaction + payment terms |
| **Risk** | Guarantee, phased rollout, exit clause |
| **Incumbent** | Switch cost analysis + migration plan |
| **Security** | SOC2, DPA, data handling appendix |
| **Scope creep** | Change order process in SOW |

### RFP response protocol

1. **Compliance matrix** — answer every requirement (Y/P/N + comment)
2. **Win themes** — 3 differentiators repeated in every section
3. **Ghost the competitor** — contrast without naming (if policy allows)
4. **Executive summary** — written last, read first
5. **Pricing** — value-anchored, not cost-plus

**Output tag:** `<proposal>` or `<rfp_response>`
