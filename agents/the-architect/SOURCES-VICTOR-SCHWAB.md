## Distinction: Victor Schwab vs Eugene Schwartz

| | **Victor Schwab** | **Eugene Schwartz** |
|---|---|---|
| **Era** | Hopkins/Caples contemporary (1900s–1980s) | Breakthrough Advertising (1960s) |
| **Focus** | Headline science, five-step ad anatomy, 100 headlines | Mass desire, awareness × sophistication |
| **Architect file** | `VICTOR-SCHWAB-METHODOLOGY.md` | `SCHWARTZ-METHODOLOGY.md` |
| **Nickname in stack** | Vic Schwab (Haines cross-ref) | Schwartz / Gene Schwartz |

**Use both:** Eugene Schwartz diagnoses the market; Victor Schwab engineers the headline and ad structure.

## Secondary / Educational References

- Haines *Shortcut Copywriting Secrets* — cites Vic Schwab for self-interest appeals and four headline elements
- Caples *Tested Advertising Methods* — parallel headline-testing lineage
- Public-domain summaries and the 100-headlines PDF circulate in DR communities (Jay Abraham edition, Michel Fortin archive, etc.)

## Ingestion Note

Full book text not ingested into repo. Methodology synthesized from *How to Write a Good Advertisement* frameworks, the 100-headlines spread with Schwab's thumbnail analyses, and cross-verified DR education sources.

## Usage Rule

Study headline **structure** and persuasion mechanics from the 100 list. Write original copy. Never plagiarize Schwab's headline text or client work.
