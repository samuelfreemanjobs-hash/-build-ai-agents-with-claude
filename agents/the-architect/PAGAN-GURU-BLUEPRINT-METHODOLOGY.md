## Guru Blueprint in One Sentence

> **Survey what they fear and desire, teach your best material for free in structured bites, sequence every piece Reptilian → Limbic → Neocortex and Why → What → How → What If — so paid products feel like the inevitable complete system.**

## Core doctrines

### 1. Education-Based Marketing (EBM)

Marketing is not interruption — it is **teaching that earns permission to sell**.

| Old world | Guru Blueprint |
|---|---|
| Ads convince strangers | Content demonstrates expertise |
| Hide best stuff for paid | Move the free line — give best technique away |
| Sales page is separate from product | **Product IS the marketing** — excerpts sell the whole |
| Pitch first | Teach first, offer second |

**Architect law:** Every content plan row must answer: *What do they learn? What do they do in 10 minutes? What trackable step next?*

### 2. Moving the Free Line

Give away the technique that gets **"Wow!"** fastest — not table scraps.

| Scarcity mindset (cut) | Free line move (ship) |
|---|---|
| "If I give this away, they won't buy" | "If I give this away, they MUST buy the system" |
| Generic tips | Named framework + one complete install |
| Tease without delivery | Full micro-transformation in the free piece |

**Pagan rule:** The paid product is the **organized, complete, supported** version of what free content samples.

**Freeman Intelligence application:** Factory chapter frameworks → LinkedIn carousels → lead magnet installs → Kindle ascension.

### 3. Product IS the Marketing

While you build the product (book, course, cohort), **creation becomes the content calendar**.

```
Write Chapter 3 (Mechanism Design)
    ↓
Extract: 1 thread · 1 carousel · 1 email · 1 lead magnet worksheet
    ↓
Each piece is a SAMPLE of the book — not random hot takes
```

**Rule:** 70%+ of scheduled content should trace to an active product spine (factory book, backend course, RSE trilogy).

## The Three-Brain Content Stack

**Never lead with analytics.** Sequence for how humans actually evaluate information:

| Order | Brain | Job | Copy/content behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Reptilian** | Survive — pay attention or ignore | Novelty, danger, curiosity, pattern interrupt — first 3–8 seconds |
| **2** | **Limbic** | Feel — is this for me? | Story, emotion, identification, peer bonding |
| **3** | **Neocortex** | Think — is this true/useful? | Framework, data, steps, proof, install |

```
REPTILIAN HOOK → LIMBIC STORY → NEOCORTEX FRAMEWORK → 10-MIN INSTALL → CTA
```

**Fail signal:** LinkedIn post opens with "Here are 7 steps…" (neocortex first — scroll past).

## The Four Learning Styles (complete communication)

Based on Kolb — address all four in every **content module** or **course unit** (email, video, chapter section, carousel):

| Style | Question they ask | You deliver |
|---|---|---|
| **WHY** | Why should I care? | Benefits, pain avoided, urgency, identity shift |
| **WHAT** | What is this? | Theory, model, distinction, named framework |
| **HOW** | How do I do it? | Exact steps — what, when, how; no interpretation needed |
| **WHAT IF** | What if I try now? | Immediate action + success indicators ("You'll know it worked when…") |

**Pagan rule:** 75% misunderstanding happens when you only teach one style. **WHY-only** = hype. **WHAT-only** = lecture. **HOW-only** = recipe without motivation. **WHAT IF missing** = no behavior change.

## 10 × 7 Curriculum Architecture

Structure **products and content series** around emotionally driven needs:

| Layer | Spec |
|---|---|
| **10 chapters / pillars** | Based on customer's strongest fears, frustrations, wants, secret dreams — NOT your expertise categories |
| **7–10 bullets per chapter** | Each bullet = one insight + one action step |
| **Order** | Highest emotional impact first, then logical learning sequence |
| **Novelty** | New frameworks perceived as more valuable — wrap need-to-know in fresh names |
| **Expansion** | Each bullet scales to: story + rationale + detail + install (full content module) |

**Maps to:** 12-chapter Kindle factory books (10 teaching pillars + intro + close), 90-day content plans, cohort modules.

## Content Module Design (atomic unit)

Every blog post, email, video, or carousel = one **module**:

| Section | Learning style | Brain |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | WHY tease | Reptilian |
| **Story / identification** | WHY deepen | Limbic |
| **Framework** | WHAT | Neocortex |
| **Steps** | HOW | Neocortex |
| **Exercise** | WHAT IF | Neocortex + behavior |
| **CTA** | Ascension | Client attraction |

**Self-contained rule:** Module must make sense alone — but **framework context** links to larger system (book, course, mechanism name).

## Idealized Design (content plan start)

Before calendar filling:

1. Close eyes — envision **ideal reader state** 90 days after consuming your content
2. Work backward — what must they believe, do, and feel each month?
3. Map 10 emotional needs (chapter pillars)
4. Assign one **free line move** per month (best install given away)
5. Build calendar from product spine — not random topics
