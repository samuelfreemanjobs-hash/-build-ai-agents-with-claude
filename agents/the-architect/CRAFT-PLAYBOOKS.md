# Craft Playbooks — Genre Structures

Use the playbook that matches the asset. Combine with SYSTEM.md workflow and EDITOR-PASSES.md.

---

## Email Sequence (5–7 emails)

| # | Job | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook + story | Enquirer subject; Star-Story-Solution body; soft CTA |
| 2 | Problem deep-dive | PAS; agitate cost of inaction |
| 3 | Mechanism reveal | Why alternatives fail; introduce new mechanism |
| 4 | Proof stack | Case studies, stats, demo |
| 5 | Offer + stack | Value equation, bonuses, guarantee |
| 6 | Objection crusher | FAQ format; pre-handle top 3 objections |
| 7 | Close | Urgency (if true); final story; hard CTA |

**Subject line:** T1 Cosmo or Enquirer energy. Test 5 variants per send.

---

## Sales Page (long-form)

1. **Pre-head** (optional qualifier: "For [avatar] who [situation]")
2. **Headline** (T1 promise, expanded)
3. **Lead** (story or problem — 150–300 words)
4. **Problem agitation** (cost of staying stuck)
5. **Mechanism** (the "aha" — why this is different)
6. **Proof section** (stacked: story → stat → authority)
7. **Offer reveal** (what they get, module by module)
8. **Value stack** (bonuses, total value, price anchor)
9. **Guarantee** (risk reversal)
10. **CTA block** (repeat 2–3 times through page)
11. **FAQ / objections**
12. **Final CTA** (identity close: "Join the [identity] who…")

## VSL Script (video sales letter)

- **0:00–0:30** — Pattern interrupt hook (Enquirer energy)
- **0:30–2:00** — Story or shocking stat
- **2:00–5:00** — Problem + failed solutions
- **5:00–10:00** — Mechanism reveal
- **10:00–15:00** — Proof parade
- **15:00–20:00** — Offer + stack
- **20:00+** — CTA + guarantee + urgency

Write for the ear. Short sentences. Stage directions for visuals/demos.

## Book Chapter (T4)

1. **Chapter hook** (story or provocative question)
2. **Promise** (what they will know/do by end)
3. **Big Idea** (one sentence)
4. **The Model** (3–7 part framework, named)
5. **The Distinction** (what experts do differently)
6. **Demonstration** (case study or worked example)
7. **Installation** (exercise, ≤10 min)
8. **Chapter close** (open loop to next chapter)

**Kindle notes:** 2,000–4,000 words per chapter; end with momentum into next chapter.

## Course Lesson (Teach mode)

Follow Pagan/Woodsmall format from SYSTEM.md.

Add:
- **Recall checkpoint** (3 questions from prior lesson)
- **Why this lesson now** (sequence logic)
- **Common failure mode** (what happens if they skip this)
- **Success criteria** (how they know they installed it)

## Facebook / Paid Ad (T2)

**Structure:**
- Hook (first line visible) — T1 energy
- Problem or curiosity (1–2 lines)
- Mechanism tease (1 line)
- CTA

**Variants:** 5 hooks × 2 bodies = 10 ads minimum for testing.

---

## Lead Magnet / Opt-in Page

- Headline: specific outcome + timeframe
- Subhead: mechanism or qualifier
- 3–5 bullets: what they get (benefit-led, not feature-led)
- CTA button: verb + outcome ("Get the 7-Step Install")
- Below fold: proof or sample screenshot

---

## Manifesto / Movement Piece (Purple Cow)

1. Name the enemy (status quo, old way, broken belief)
2. Name the new game (category reframe)
3. Tell the origin story (why you see what others miss)
4. Present the manifesto (5–10 principles, punchy)
5. Invitation (who this is for; who it is not for)
6. Call to assemble (community, waitlist, movement)
