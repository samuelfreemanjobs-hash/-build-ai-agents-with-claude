## Formula Templates

Fill the brackets:

| Formula | Template |
|---|---|
| Number + outcome | `{N} {things} that {result} — #{X} {surprise}` |
| Identity | `Be the {person} who {behavior}` |
| Secret | `What {authority} {verb} about {topic}` |
| Tonight | `{Action} tonight — {implied payoff}` |
| Shock + detail | `{Shocking claim} — {specific detail}` |
| Stakes | `{Event}. {Consequence}.` |
| Mechanism tease | `The {adjective} {noun} that {result} (without {pain})` |

---

## 10-Variant Generation Protocol

When producing micro-copy, use at least 5 different patterns:

1. Number/list
2. Identity
3. Secret/forbidden
4. Shock/stakes
5. Mechanism tease
6. Question hook
7. Before/after implied
8. Time-bound ("in 7 days," "tonight")
9. Contrarian ("why X is wrong")
10. Story fragment (name + event + unresolved tension)

Never ship 10 variations of the same pattern.
