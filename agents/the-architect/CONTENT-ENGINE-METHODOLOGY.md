## Content Engine in One Sentence

> **One master asset becomes ten platform-native pieces — authority, proof, story, and promo rotating on a 30-day calendar that feeds the funnel — every piece designed as a Guru Blueprint module (teach first, sample the product, install in 10 minutes).**

---

## Guru Blueprint content design (default)

Before repurposing, architect the **master module** (`PAGAN-GURU-BLUEPRINT-METHODOLOGY.md`):

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| **Education-Based Marketing** | Teach → trust → offer (not pitch-first) |
| **Move the Free Line** | Give best technique free; paid = complete system |
| **Product = Marketing** | Factory chapter / course module = content source |
| **Three-Brain Stack** | Reptilian hook → limbic story → neocortex framework |
| **Four Learning Styles** | Why · What · How · What If in every module |
| **10×7 Pillars** | Emotional needs → chapter titles → calendar months |

**Weekly rhythm:** W1 WHY · W2 WHAT · W3 HOW · W4 WHAT IF + CTA

## The 1 → 10+ Content Engine

One long-form master (essay, podcast, VSL breakdown, training) spawns:

| # | Asset type |
|---|---|
| 1 | X/Twitter thread (hook + 8–12 tweets) |
| 2 | LinkedIn carousel or long post |
| 3 | Instagram/TikTok short (60–90 sec hook) |
| 4 | YouTube short clip |
| 5 | Email to list (story + CTA) |
| 6 | Quote card / screenshot line |
| 7 | Contrarian hot take post |
| 8 | Case study micro-story |
| 9 | FAQ/objection post |
| 10 | Direct promo CTA post |

**Rule:** Extract **structure**, rewrite for platform — never lazy cross-post identical copy.

## Four Content Pillars (30-Day Rotation)

| Pillar | Job | % of calendar |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority / Educational** | Teach mechanism; Results in Advance | ~35% |
| **Proof / Case Studies** | Social proof; specific results | ~25% |
| **Personal / Polarizing** | Origin myth, war stories, enemy | ~25% |
| **Direct Promo** | Offer, deadline, CTA | ~15% |

**Kennedy:** Never promo-only — magnetic ratio earns promo right.

---

## Platform-Native Tone (Same Voice, Different Compression)

| Platform | Format | Architect adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| **X/Twitter** | Punchy threads; contrarian open | T1 hooks; Enquirer energy |
| **Instagram/TikTok** | Visual hook in 3 sec; story arc | Cinematic open; one install |
| **LinkedIn** | Framework breakdown; professional peer | Teaching chunk; no guru stiffness |
| **YouTube** | Deep dive; chapters; retention hooks | T3–T4; slippery slide sections |
| **Email** | Personal letter; one idea | Collier conversation; A-Pile |
| **Podcast** | Story → framework → CTA soft | Bonding + mechanism |

**Celebrity:** Catchphrases and visual signature consistent across platforms.

## Viral & Algorithm Mechanics

| Tactic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Pattern interrupt** | First line breaks scroll autopilot |
| **Polarization** | Clear stance — some disagree (Godin purple) |
| **Trendjacking** | Mechanism applied to current event |
| **Open loops** | Thread/post ends mid-story |
| **Specific numbers** | Hopkins/Ogilvy — credibility in feed |
| **Identity hooks** | Cosmo — "smart [avatar] who…" |

**Measurable:** Track saves, shares, replies — remarkability rubric.

## Content → Funnel Integration

Every content piece maps to funnel stage:

| Content | Funnel job |
|---|---|
| Educational | Belief / indoctrination |
| Proof | Convert warm traffic |
| Personal story | Bonding / opt-in |
| Promo | Direct to offer |
| Lead magnet CTA | Capture |

**No orphan content** — every asset has metric: opt-ins, clicks, or sales.

## 30-Day Campaign Map (Template Structure)

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Authority | Personal story | Proof |
| **2** | Educational | Polarizing take | Soft CTA |
| **3** | Case study | Mechanism deep-dive | Promo |
| **4** | Authority | War story | Hard CTA + urgency |

Adjust frequency per platform. Batch-create from one master asset per week.
