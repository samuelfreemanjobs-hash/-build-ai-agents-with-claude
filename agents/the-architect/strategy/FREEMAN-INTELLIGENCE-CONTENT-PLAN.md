## Three-brain + four styles (default post structure)

Every LinkedIn / email follows:

```
REPTILIAN: One-line pattern interrupt
LIMBIC: 3-sentence peer story
NEOCORTEX: Named framework (3 bullets max)
HOW: Numbered steps (specific)
WHAT IF: "Do this in the next 10 minutes…"
CTA: One trackable action
```
