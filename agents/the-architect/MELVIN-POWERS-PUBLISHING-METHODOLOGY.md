## Powers in One Sentence

## The Powers Revenue Stack

```
ACCOUNTABLE AD (Kindle · classified · keyed display)
        ↓
FREE DETAILS / LOW-FRICTION ENTRY (lead magnet · sample chapter · $0.99 tripwire)
        ↓
BUYER LIST (email = modern mail-order list — names who PAID or opted with intent)
        ↓
BOOK SALE (high margin · calling card · proof of authority)
        ↓
BACKEND (seminar · cohort · consulting · course · next book · licensing)
        ↓
BACKLIST (catalog compounds · every book sells every other book)
```

**Powers law:** Front-end book profit is nice. **Backend + backlist** is the business.

## Front-End Book Economics (Powers markup discipline)

| Element | Target |
|---|---|
| **Production cost** | Factory/automation minimizes marginal cost per title |
| **Front-end price** | Kindle $9.99–$24.99 + **direct bundle premium** |
| **True goal** | Cost per **buyer** acquired — not royalty per unit |
| **Break-even** | List growth + backend pitch — book can break even or loss-lead |
| **Winner test** | EPC, attach rate to backend, LTV — not Amazon rank alone |

**Powers insight:** A book that loses money on paper but builds 10,000 buyers is a **win**.

## Testing Protocol (Powers × Hopkins)

```
1. RUN    — smallest viable traffic (500-name mail test · $50 ad test)
2. MEASURE — response rate, CPA, EPC, backend attach
3. CHANGE — ONE variable only (headline · price · guarantee · bonus)
4. SCALE  — winners only; kill losers fast
5. ARCHIVE — document in factory diagnostics
```

**Split-run mindset:** Two headlines, same list half-and-half — not gut feel.
