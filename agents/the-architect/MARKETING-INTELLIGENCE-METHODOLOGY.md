# Marketing Intelligence & Revenue Discovery Methodology

**Phase 1 intelligence layer for The Architect.** Revenue opportunity discovery, qualitative VoC mining, quantitative funnel analytics, and competitive intelligence — assembled before strategy, copy, or AI system design.

**One voice.** Not a separate analyst persona. Data informs the same author who writes the sales page.

**Template:** `research/GALACTIC-EXECUTIVE-DIAGNOSTIC-TEMPLATE.md` (sections 1A–1B)

---

## Intelligence in One Sentence

> **Find where money is hiding — in white space competitors ignore, in words customers already use, and in funnel leaks your dashboard already shows — then channel it onto one mechanism and one metric.**

---

## The Three Intelligence Lenses

| Lens | Job | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue Discovery** | Where to play; what to monetize next | Opportunity map + monetization multipliers |
| **Marketing Research** | What they say, feel, fear, want | VoC lexicon + desire map |
| **Marketing Analytics** | What numbers prove | Benchmark sheet + drop-off diagnosis |

## Revenue Opportunity & Discovery Intel

### Market arbitrage & white space

| Scan | Questions |
|---|---|
| **Competitor gaps** | What do reviews complain about? What do ads over-promise? |
| **Channel arbitrage** | Where is CAC low but competitors aren't? (DM, podcast, niche platform) |
| **Category blue ocean** | New ladder rung (Ries) — own a word no one owns |
| **Sub-segment neglect** | Smallest viable audience (Godin) competitors ignore |

### Monetization multipliers

| Multiplier | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Upsell pathways** | OTO, bump, application funnel, DFY tier |
| **Recurring loops** | Membership, retainer, consumable replenishment |
| **Backend extensions** | Mastermind, certification, licensing, agency |
| **Hidden assets** | Email list, content library, data, community (Abraham) |

**Abraham link:** Power Parthenon — each multiplier = new pillar.

## Qualitative VoC Mining

**Deep file:** `VOC-RESEARCH.md`

| Source | Extract |
|---|---|
| Forum threads / Reddit / comments | Pain phrases, failed solutions, slang |
| Customer service logs | Verbatim complaints, refund reasons |
| Competitor reviews (1–3 star) | Gap language, unmet expectations |
| Sales call transcripts | Objections, buying triggers, decision language |
| Social DMs / support tickets | Real-time anxiety vocabulary |

**Output:** VoC lexicon — minimum 20 verbatim phrases tagged by emotion (fear, desire, anger, frustration).

**Collier link:** Enter the mental conversation already in progress — use their exact words.

## Quantitative Marketing Analysis

| Metric | Benchmark use |
|---|---|
| **CAC** | Channel efficiency; scale/kill decisions |
| **ROAS / MER** | Paid acquisition health |
| **LTV** | Backend viability; CAC ceiling |
| **CVR by stage** | Funnel leak identification |
| **AOV** | Stack and bump effectiveness |
| **Churn / retention cohorts** | Product-market fit signal |
| **Payback period** | Cash flow risk |
| **Pipeline velocity** | B2B sales cycle health |

### Funnel drop-off diagnosis

```
Traffic → Opt-in → Engagement → Add-to-cart → Purchase → Ascension
```

For each stage: **rate**, **benchmark**, **hypothesis**, **fix asset**.

**Hopkins link:** Track everything. Test one variable. Kill losers.

## Competitive Intelligence Sweep

| Element | Capture |
|---|---|
| Top 5 competitors | Offer, price, mechanism, guarantee, funnel type |
| Ad library | Headlines, hooks, angles running longest |
| Sophistication stage | What claims are exhausted? |
| Mechanism gaps | What HOW is unclaimed? |
| Proof types | What evidence do they stack? |

**Schwartz link:** Sophistication diagnosis drives headline and mechanism strategy.

---

## Intelligence Workflow

```
1. DEFINE     — Avatar, offer, metric, channel
2. DISCOVER   — White space + monetization multipliers
3. MINE       — VoC qualitative (20+ phrases)
4. MEASURE    — Quantitative benchmarks + funnel stages
5. COMPETE    — Competitive sweep + sophistication tag
6. SYNTHESIZE — Opportunity brief → Phase 1 diagnostic summary
```

Save to: `projects/<slug>/diagnostics/`

---

## Ship Gate (Intelligence)

- [ ] VoC lexicon ≥ 20 verbatim phrases
- [ ] At least one monetization multiplier identified
- [ ] Competitive sophistication stage assigned
- [ ] Funnel stage metrics or assumptions stated
- [ ] White space / arbitrage hypothesis documented
