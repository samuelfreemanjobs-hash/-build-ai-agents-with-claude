| Skill | What it means | PM application |
|---|---|---|
| **4 Ps audit** | Product · Price · Place · Promotion — all four planned together | Monthly marketing-ops plan: never promo without price/place fit |

### Martin Fishbein — Theory of Reasoned Action

**Purchase requires:** motivation + perceived advantages + emotional readiness — routed through beliefs and intentions.

| Component | Fishbein | Copy/funnel equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Attitude** | Beliefs × evaluation of outcome | Mechanism + proof stack |
| **Subjective norm** | What peers expect / approve | Social proof · tribe (Godin) · testimonials |
| **Perceived control** | "I can actually do this" | Quick win install (Pagan) · guarantee · simplicity |
| **Intention** | Committed to act | Micro-yes sequence · cart · application |

**Fishbein funnel audit (before launch):**

1. Are they **motivated**? (Schwartz desire + Kennedy agitation)
2. Do they see **advantages**? (Hormozi value equation)
3. Do they feel **excited**? (Sugarman emotion · Jobs drama)
4. Is **intention** clear? (One CTA · no confusion)

**Gap to add:** `research/FISHBEIN-REASONED-ACTION-TEMPLATE.md` for launch audits.
