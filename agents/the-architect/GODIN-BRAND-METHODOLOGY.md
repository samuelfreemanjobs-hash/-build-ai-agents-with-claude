# Seth Godin Brand Methodology

Remarkability, tribes, permission, and smallest-viable-audience branding for The Architect. **One voice** — Godin supplies **who the brand is for**, **why they'd miss you if gone**, and **how permission replaces interruption**.

**Core texts:** *Purple Cow* · *Tribes* · *This Is Marketing* · *Permission Marketing* · *The Dip*

**Sources:** See `SOURCES-BRANDING.md`

---

## Godin in One Sentence

> **Don't find customers for your product — find products for your customers. Make something worth noticing, lead a tribe, earn permission, then ship.**

---

## The Godin Thesis

| Myth | Godin reality |
|---|---|
| Marketing = advertising | Marketing = **change** — help people become who they seek to become |
| Everyone is your customer | **Smallest viable audience** — specific people, deeply served |
| Interrupt to win | **Permission** — anticipated, personal, relevant messages |
| Safe is smart | **Safe is risky** — invisible = dead |
| More features = better | **Remarkable** = worth remarking about |

**Architect improvement:** Purple Cow must be **measurable** — share rate, referral CTA, quote-ability, screenshot test (QUALITY-RUBRIC dimension 6). Remarkability without conversion is art, not brand.

## Purple Cow (Remarkability)

In a field of brown cows, **purple is the only strategy.**

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Screenshot test** | Would someone send this to a friend? |
| **Argument test** | Would some people hate this? (Polarity — Celebrity layer) |
| **Category test** | Does this change how they think about the problem? |
| **Boring test** | Could any competitor publish this unchanged? |

**Ries link:** Purple Cow often **creates the new category** — first purple cow owns the ladder.

**Schwartz stage 5:** Identification marketing = tribe + personality (Godin + Celebrity).

## Smallest Viable Audience (SVA)

> *"Everyone is not your customer."*

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **Name them** | Specific psychographic — not "small business owners" |
| **Wish for them** | What change do they seek? (Core Identity — Kern) |
| **Exclude** | Who this is NOT for — Kennedy repulsion |
| **Serve deeply** | Product/copy only for SVA — sacrifice scale early |
| **Find peers** | SVA members know each other — word of mouth |

**Halbert:** Starving crowd within SVA — urgent + reachable.

## Tribes (Leadership Brand)

A tribe = **shared interest + communication + leader.**

| Element | Brand job |
|---|---|
| **Shared interest** | Category word + enemy (Ries + Godin) |
| **Communication** | Ritual content — email, community, show |
| **Leader** | You — not hero, **leader** (Campbell mentor) |
| **Movement** | Manifesto, us vs them, status of belonging |

**Celebrity:** Tribe leader = celebrity architecture end state.

## Permission Marketing

| Interruption (reject) | Permission (Godin) |
|---|---|
| Cold spam | Opt-in with anticipated value |
| Bait-and-switch | Results in Advance (Kern) |
| Frequency without value | Consistent gift → offer |
| One-shot blast | Sequence that earns next open |

**Three permissions:** Anticipated · Personal · Relevant

**Kern IBB:** Pre-frame → indoctrinate → convert = permission ladder.

**Cialdini:** Reciprocity seeds permission.

## This Is Marketing (Modern Stack)

Godin's five-step marketing workflow:

```
1. Invent a thing worth making (with a story)
2. Design and build so few people benefit greatly
3. Tell a story that matches the built-in narrative
4. Spread the word (not spam — remarkability)
5. Show up consistently — lead the tribe
```

**Info-Product Engine:** Step 1–2 = transformation ladder + SVA. Step 3–5 = launch + permission sequences.

## The Dip

**Quit the right things** — don't dilute brand climbing wrong ladder.

| Strategic quit | Strategic persist |
|---|---|
| Wrong audience | Right tribe through the hard middle |
| Wrong category | Category you can lead |
| Line extension | Focus on owned word |
| Generic offer | Remarkable mechanism |

**Ries sacrifice + Godin dip** = brand focus discipline.

## Godin Checklist
