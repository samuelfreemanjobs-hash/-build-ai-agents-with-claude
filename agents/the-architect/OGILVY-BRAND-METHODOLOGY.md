# David Ogilvy Brand Methodology

Brand image, consistent personality, and persuasive prestige for The Architect. **One voice** — Ogilvy supplies **how the brand looks, sounds, and feels** across every touchpoint while remaining salesmanship — not decoration.

**Core text:** *Ogilvy on Advertising* · Ogilvy & Mather doctrine · Rolls-Royce, Hathaway, Schweppes campaigns

**Sources:** See `SOURCES-BRANDING.md`

---

## Ogilvy in One Sentence

> **Every advertisement is part of the long-term investment in the personality of the brand — and the consumer is not a moron; she is your wife.**

---

## The Ogilvy Thesis

| Myth | Ogilvy reality |
|---|---|
| Brand image = fluffy awareness | **Image is cumulative** — every ad deposits or withdraws |
| Creativity wins awards | **Salesmanship wins** — test, measure, repeat winners |
| Short-term promo vs brand | Great campaigns do **both** — personality + reason-why |
| Facts bore people | **Specific facts build prestige** — Rolls-Royce clock ad |
| Change campaigns often | **Consistency** — 20+ years for Hathaway man |

**Architect improvement:** Every brand touchpoint must pass **Victor Schwab select + reward** — image without hook is invisible. Ogilvy prestige + Schwab flag.

## The Brand Image Doctrine

> *"The personality of a product is an amalgam of many things — its name, its packaging, its price, its style of advertising, and, above all, the nature of the product itself."*

| Deposit | Withdrawal |
|---|---|
| Consistent visual + verbal identity | Random fonts, tones, promises |
| Specific, believable claims | Superlatives without proof |
| Premium cues aligned with price | Discount brand voice on premium offer |
| Long-running character/device | Disposable campaign gimmicks |
| Service to customer | Hype that insults intelligence |

**Hopkins alignment:** Specificity is prestige. "At 60 miles an hour the loudest noise comes from the electric clock."

## Ogilvy's Operational Rules (Brand-Critical)

### On headlines
- **Five times as many read headline as body** — invest disproportionately
- **Headline promises benefit** — self-interest (Caples/Schwab alignment)
- **News in headline** when true — "New," "Now," "Introducing"
- **Localize** when relevant — city name in headline lifts response
- **Long headlines** can outpull short when they promise real news

### On visuals
- **Story appeal in photo** — eye travels to human face, curiosity
- **Before/after** when ethical and provable
- **Captions under photos** — twice as many read as body copy
- **No irrelevant creativity** — every element sells

### On copy
- **Write to one person** — Halbert A-Pile, Collier conversation
- **Don't insult intelligence** — explain; reason-why
- **Long copy sells** when product warrants it and interest is earned
- **Repeat winners** — winning campaign runs for years (Hathaway, Rolls-Royce)

### On brand building
- **Give product a personality** — distinctive character over time
- **Use a consistent device** — eyepatch, color, spokesperson, format
- **Premium brands need premium tone** — price is part of image
- **Corporate advertising rarely sells** — product brand sells

## The Big Idea (Ogilvy)

Every sustained brand campaign rests on **one big idea** — not a new idea every quarter.

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Durability** | Can this run 5–20 years? |
| **Distinctiveness** | Could a competitor run this unchanged? |
| **Believability** | Specific proof supports it? |
| **Salesmanship** | Would this help close face-to-face? |

**Architect:** One big idea = SYSTEM.md ship gate. Ogilvy + Halbert + Schwartz alignment.

---

## Brand Personality Dimensions

Define once; enforce in every asset:

| Dimension | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Voice** | Peer mentor vs authority vs rebel (Architect default: peer-cinematic) |
| **Vocabulary** | Words we use / words we never use |
| **Visual** | Color, typography, photo style, layout rhythm |
| **Character** | Recurring figure, mascot, or founder face |
| **Ritual** | Format readers recognize (newsletter name, show structure) |
| **Price signal** | Premium, accessible, or challenger |

**Celebrity Layer 3:** Signature system = Ogilvy personality operationalized.

## Ogilvy Checklist

- [ ] Headline = benefit or news (not clever vanity)
- [ ] Specific facts, not superlatives
- [ ] Visual + caption sell (not decorate)
- [ ] Personality consistent with prior assets
- [ ] Big idea identifiable in one sentence
- [ ] Consumer treated as intelligent
- [ ] Long copy justified by interest earned
- [ ] CTA present — image ads still convert
