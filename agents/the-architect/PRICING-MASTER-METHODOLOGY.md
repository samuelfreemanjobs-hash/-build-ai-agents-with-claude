## Value-Based Pricing (Not Cost-Plus)

| Cost-plus (reject) | Value-based (Architect) |
|---|---|
| Hours × rate | Outcome economic value |
| Margin on materials | ROI multiple for buyer |
| "Industry standard" | Category premium signal |

**Economic value formula (simplified):**

```
Price ceiling ≈ (Cost of problem × probability of solution) − risk discount
```

**Copy move:** Quantify cost of inaction (Kennedy agitation) before revealing price.

## Anchor & Decoy Architecture

### High-anchor decoy tiers

| Tier | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Anchor (decoy)** | Makes primary feel reasonable | $2,497 "Done-For-You" |
| **Primary (target)** | Where margin and volume meet | $497 "Self-Serve + Support" |
| **Entry** | Liquidates CAC or filters | $47 tripwire |

**Rule:** Decoy must be **real** — a genuine offer someone could buy. Never fake comparison.

### Stack anchoring (Hormozi + Pricing Master)

1. Sum standalone values of core + bonuses
2. Reveal stack total
3. Contrast with price ("you invest $X")
4. Ratio check ≥ 10×

## Price Elasticity Diagnostics

| Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| High CVR, low refund | Room to raise price |
| Low CVR, strong traffic | Offer or price-position problem — test stack before discount |
| High cart abandon at price reveal | Anchor or payment terms weak |
| Backend ascension weak | Front-end underpriced or wrong avatar |

**Test discipline:** One variable — price OR terms OR stack — never all at once (Hopkins/Caples).

## Payment Psychology & Term Structuring

| Structure | When | Psychology |
|---|---|---|
| **Full pay discount** | Cash flow priority | Reward commitment; reduce churn risk |
| **Split pay (2–3)** | $500+ offers | Reduces sticker shock; increases CVR |
| **Micro-installments** | Mass market | "Less than coffee per day" reframe |
| **Trial / pilot** | High skepticism | Reduces perceived likelihood denominator (Hormozi) |
| **Usage-based** | SaaS / tools | Aligns price with value received |
| **Annual vs monthly** | Subscriptions | Annual = F lever (Abraham); monthly = lower barrier |

**Kennedy link:** Make saying no feel stupid — terms are part of the offer.

## Premium vs Commodity Signals

| Commodity | Premium |
|---|---|
| Hourly / generic package | Named outcome-based offer |
| Discount-led | Value-stack-led |
| Single price | Tiered architecture with anchor |
| Weak guarantee | Named aggressive guarantee |
| Compared on features | Compared on transformation + speed + certainty |

**Schwartz stage 3+:** Mechanism name justifies premium.  
**Ries:** Own the category word — price supports position.

## Pricing Workflow

```
1. VALUE     — Economic value of outcome (quantified)
2. STACK     — Hormozi Grand Slam build
3. ANCHOR    — Decoy tier + stack total
4. PRIMARY   — Target price (margin + CVR model)
5. TERMS     — Full pay / split / trial
6. ELASTICITY — Hypothesis + test plan
7. COPY      — Anchor reveal sequence in sales asset
```
