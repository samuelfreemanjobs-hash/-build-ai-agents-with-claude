# Swipe File — Annotated Examples

Reference library for The Architect. When drafting, match **structure and psychology** — never plagiarize. Adapt patterns to the user's niche, voice, and proof.

**How to use:**
1. Identify awareness level + genre + pattern needed.
2. Find 2–3 annotated examples below.
3. Extract the **mechanism** (why it works), not just the words.
4. Write original copy using the same structural moves.

## Leads (Opening 150–300 words)

### L-001 — Story Lead
**Copy (excerpt):**
> *If you or someone you care about suffers from joint pain, I need to tell you about a conversation I overheard in a grocery store last Tuesday...*

**Tags:** AW-P | GEN-L | PT-Story
**Why it works:** Enters through eavesdropped scene. Reader is voyeur. Pain named early; story delivers proof.
**Structural move:** [Condition] → [overheard scene] → [unexpected discovery]

### L-002 — Problem-Agitate Lead
**Copy (excerpt):**
> *You're working harder than ever. Revenue is flat. Your competitors — the ones with half your talent — are somehow winning. And the worst part? You can't figure out why.*

**Tags:** AW-P | GEN-L | PT-PAS
**Why it works:** Second person. Triple agitation (effort, flat results, inferior competitors). "Worst part" escalates.
**Structural move:** [Effort without reward] → [unfair comparison] → [mystery/agitation peak]

### L-003 — Mechanism Lead
**Copy (excerpt):**
> *There's a reason your ads aren't converting. It's not your offer. It's not your traffic. It's something nobody in the Facebook groups is talking about — because most of them don't know it exists.*

**Tags:** AW-S | GEN-L | PT-Mech+Contrarian
**Why it works:** Eliminates obvious answers. Creates new category (hidden mechanism). Positions reader as insider-to-be.
**Structural move:** [Failed diagnosis] → [eliminate obvious] → [name hidden mechanism]

### L-004 — Star-Story-Solution Lead
**Copy (excerpt):**
> *Three years ago I was $80k in debt, sleeping on a friend's couch, wondering if I'd wasted my 30s on a "business" that would never work. Today I'm writing this from a house I own outright. The difference wasn't a hack. It was one distinction I learned from a bankrupt copywriter in a Denny's at 2am.*

**Tags:** AW-P | GEN-L | PT-Story+Mech
**Why it works:** Before/after with specific numbers. Origin story with scene (Denny's, 2am). Mechanism teased, not revealed.
**Structural move:** [Rock bottom specifics] → [transformation] → [unlikely mentor/scene] → [mechanism tease]

## Teaching Openers (Pagan/Woodsmall)

**Copy:** *"Most people think marketing is about getting attention. It's not. Marketing is about installing a belief sequence that makes the right action feel inevitable. That distinction — attention vs. belief — is worth more than any ad budget."*

**Structural move:** [Common belief] → [correction] → [name distinction] → [stakes]

### T-002 — Model Opener
**Copy:** *"Every offer that converts has three layers: the promise (what they get), the mechanism (why it works), and the proof (why they should believe you). Miss any layer and the whole thing collapses. Let me show you each one."*
**Tags:** GEN-L | PT-Teach
**Why it works:** 3-part model named upfront. Consequence of missing parts. Promise to demonstrate.
**Structural move:** [Named model] → [failure mode] → [teach promise]

## Pattern Extraction Cheat Sheet

When swiping, extract these elements:

| Element | Question to ask |
|---|---|
| **Hook type** | Story? Question? Contrarian? Proof? |
| **Open loop** | What is withheld? |
| **Specificity** | What numbers, times, names, scenes? |
| **Identity** | Who does the reader want to become? |
| **Enemy** | What belief, habit, or competitor is challenged? |
| **Mechanism** | What "new way" is teased? |
| **Proof type** | Stat, story, demo, authority, social? |
| **CTA friction** | What risk is removed? |
