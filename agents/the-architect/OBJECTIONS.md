# Objection Library

Pre-built rebuttals for The Architect. Match objection → select frame → adapt with user's proof and voice.

**How to use:**
1. Run `VOC-RESEARCH.md` to surface real objections from the market.
2. Map top 5 objections before any sales asset.
3. Pre-handle in copy (don't wait for FAQ only).
4. Pull rebuttal frames from this library; customize with specifics.

---

## Universal Objections (All Offers)

### O-01: "It's too expensive"

**Psychology:** Value not established. Price compared to wrong anchor.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Cost of inaction:** *"What's it costing you every month to stay stuck? If [problem] costs you $X/month, this pays for itself in [timeframe]."*
- **Reframe as investment:** *"You're not buying [product]. You're buying [transformation] — and the alternative is another year of [pain]."*
- **Stack comparison:** *"A single [alternative: agency hour / failed ad spend / month of tools] costs more than this — and doesn't include [unique mechanism]."*
- **Payment math:** *"That's $X/day — less than [relatable daily spend]. For [specific outcome]."*

**Pre-handle placement:** After value stack, before CTA.

---

### O-02: "I need to think about it"

**Psychology:** Unresolved risk, unclear next step, or polite no. Often means "I don't believe yet."

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Name the real question:** *"Totally fair. Usually when someone says that, they're wondering about [specific risk: will it work for me / is this legit / can I afford it]. Which one is it?"*
- **Install now:** *"The only thing to think about: can you afford another [timeframe] without [outcome]? The framework takes 10 minutes to start."*
- **Risk reversal:** *"Take 30 days. If it's not right, [guarantee]. Thinking about it costs you [specific ongoing pain]."*
- **Scarcity (if true):** *"Think overnight — but know [genuine limit: cohort cap / price increase / bonus expiry] closes [date]."*

**Pre-handle placement:** FAQ + near final CTA.

---

### O-03: "I've tried something like this before and it didn't work"

**Psychology:** Solution-aware skeptic. Burned before. Needs mechanism differentiation.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Validate + differentiate:** *"Of course you have — and it probably failed because [common reason old approach fails]. This works differently: [mechanism]."*
- **Not your fault:** *"The old way wasn't designed for [your situation]. That's not a character flaw — it's a category error."*
- **Proof of different:** *"[Case study: someone in exact same situation] tried [old thing], failed, then [specific result] with [mechanism] in [timeframe]."*
- **Distinction (Woodsmall):** *"The difference between [old approach] and [new approach] is [named distinction]. Once you see it, you can't unsee it."*

**Pre-handle placement:** Early in sales page (after problem agitation) + proof section.

---

### O-04: "I don't have time"

**Psychology:** Overwhelm. Fear of another failed commitment. Often a priority objection disguised.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Time cost of status quo:** *"You don't have time NOT to fix this. Every week without [solution] costs [specific: hours / dollars / stress]."*
- **Minimum viable install:** *"The core install takes [10 min / 1 hour]. Not a second job — a single session."*
- **Done-for-you element:** *"[Templates / scripts / swipe files] mean you're not starting from zero."*
- **Identity:** *"Busy [avatar] don't find time — they install systems that create time."*

**Pre-handle placement:** Bullets + FAQ.

---

### O-05: "I need to talk to my spouse/partner/boss"

**Psychology:** Real for joint decisions; sometimes stall tactic.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Equip them:** *"Here's a one-page summary you can share — covers [outcome, price, guarantee, why now]."*
- **Joint benefit:** *"What would [spouse/partner] want you to have? Usually it's [security / less stress / more income]. This delivers that."*
- **Risk removal for joint buy-in:** *"[Guarantee] means the downside is capped. The upside is [transformation]. Easy conversation."*

**Pre-handle placement:** FAQ + optional "share this" PDF mention.

---

### O-06: "Is this a scam? / Sounds too good to be true"

**Psychology:** Trust deficit. Market burned by hype.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Specificity over hype:** Replace superlatives with numbers, names, timelines.
- **Proof stack:** *"Don't take my word — [testimonial with full name], [screenshot], [third-party validation]."*
- **Reason why:** *"Here's exactly why this works when [old approach] doesn't: [mechanism]."*
- **Under-promise:** *"I'm not promising [unrealistic outcome]. I'm promising [specific, believable outcome] — and here's the proof."*

**Pre-handle placement:** Throughout; heavy in proof section.

---

### O-07: "I'll do it myself / I can find this free online"

**Psychology:** Undervalues curation, speed, and accountability.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Time arbitrage:** *"You can. It'll take [realistic hours] to piece together, test, and fail forward. Or install what took me [years / $X] in [timeframe]."*
- **Curation value:** *"Free content is scattered and contradictory. This is [sequenced / tested / updated] — the distinction between information and installation."*
- **Opportunity cost:** *"What's your hour worth? If it's $X, you've already spent more than this price researching."*

**Pre-handle placement:** Value stack + FAQ.

---

### O-08: "Now isn't the right time"

**Psychology:** Fear, overwhelm, or legitimate timing. Seasonal for some niches.

**Rebuttal frames:**
- **Cost of delay:** *"The right time is a myth. The cost of waiting [one quarter / one year] is [specific pain or lost revenue]."*
- **Start small:** *"You don't have to implement everything today. Module 1 takes [time] and delivers [quick win]."*
- **Seasonal (if true):** *"[Now] is actually when [avatar] see the best results because [reason]."*

**Pre-handle placement:** Close + email sequence email 6–7.

## Niche Objection Modules

Copy these into project-specific `OBJECTIONS-[niche].md` after VOC research.

### Coaching / Consulting

| Objection | Frame |
|---|---|
| "I already have a coach" | *"Great — this [complements / goes deeper on X]. Most coaches don't teach [mechanism]."* |
| "My market is different" | *"[Case study] said the same. [Specific result] in [their market]."* |
| "I need clients first" | *"This IS how you get clients — [mechanism] before the pitch."* |

### Info Products / Courses

| Objection | Frame |
|---|---|
| "I never finish courses" | *"Designed for installation, not binge-watching. Each module = one [10-min action]."* |
| "Too much content" | *"[X] modules. [Y] hours total. Skip to [quick-win module] first."* |
| "Will this be outdated?" | *"Principles over tactics. [Mechanism] works regardless of [platform change]."* |

### E-commerce / Physical Products

| Objection | Frame |
|---|---|
| "Cheaper on Amazon" | *"You're not buying [product] — you're buying [guarantee / support / bundle / speed]."* |
| "Shipping takes too long" | *"[Exact timeframe]. Plus [bonus for waiting / expedite option]."* |
| "Does it actually work?" | *"[Reviews / UGC / before-after]. [Guarantee]."* |

### Health / Wellness (compliance-aware)

| Objection | Frame |
|---|---|
| "Tried everything" | *"Because [common approaches] miss [mechanism]. This addresses [root cause framing — no disease claims]."* |
| "Is it safe?" | *"[Ingredients / credentials / third-party test]. Consult your physician if [condition]."* |
| "Results take too long" | *"[Realistic timeframe]. [Small win] in [shorter timeframe]."* |

**Note:** Never make unsubstantiated health claims. See compliance notes in project brief.

### B2B / High-Ticket

| Objection | Frame |
|---|---|
| "Need ROI proof" | *"[Case study]: $X invested → $Y return in [timeframe]. [Calculator / pilot option]."* |
| "Procurement / legal" | *"We provide [MSA / SOC2 / invoice / NET terms]. Happy to jump on a call with your team."* |
| "We're locked into [vendor]" | *"This integrates with / replaces [specific function] without [migration pain]."* |

## Pre-Handle Placement Guide

| Asset | Where to pre-handle |
|---|---|
| Sales page | After mechanism (O-03), in FAQ (all), before each CTA (top 2) |
| Email sequence | Dedicated email #6 = objection crusher |
| VSL | 15–18 min mark, before final offer |
| Ad | Address #1 objection in body or first comment |
| Webinar | Q&A prep + slide "What if..." |
| Book chapter | Sidebar or "Common Mistake" box |

## Objection Crusher Email Template

**Subject:** *[Objection as question — e.g. "Too expensive?"]*

**Body structure:**
1. Quote the objection verbatim (VOC language)
2. Validate: *"I get it. I thought the same."*
3. Reframe with distinction
4. Proof (story or stat)
5. Risk reversal
6. CTA
