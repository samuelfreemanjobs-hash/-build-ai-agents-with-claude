## 7-Step checklist

- [ ] **Attract** — offer promotable (proof, mechanism, swipes ready)
- [ ] **Identify** — IPP scored; top 10–50 partners listed
- [ ] **Initiate** — gave value first (promoted their work, engaged, intro)
- [ ] **Invite** — proposal sent (dates, commission, effort)
- [ ] **Equip** — partner pack complete (`JV-PARTNER-PACK-TEMPLATE.md`)
- [ ] **Execute** — coordinated mail calendar; launch week support
- [ ] **Celebrate** — results shared; next collab scheduled
