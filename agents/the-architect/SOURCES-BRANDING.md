## Architect Improvements (documented in synthesis)

1. **Brand Conversion Doctrine** — mind share must convert (Schwab select+reward on every touch)
2. **Measurable Purple Cow** — remarkability rubric, not vague "be different"
3. **Ownable search terms** — Ries word + named mechanism (Google-able category)
4. **5P Loop** — Position → Personify → Permit → Prove → Profit (continuous optimization)
5. **Three-gate audit** — Ries + Ogilvy/Schwab + Godin before ship

## Ingestion Note

Synthesized from published frameworks and campaign canon. Full texts not ingested into repo. Cross-verified against existing Architect methodologies to avoid contradiction.

## Usage Rule

Extract **structure and principles**. Write original brand strategy and copy for each client/project. Never plagiarize campaign copy from Ogilvy archives or Godin/Ries book prose.
