## The Partnership Pyramid

| Tier | Relationship | Commission / value | Your job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Affiliate** | Low-touch; swipe copy | 30–50% | Turn-key pack; tracking; compliance |
| **2 — JV Partner** | Coordinated launch window | 40–50% + bonuses | Pre-launch relationship + exclusive partner stack |
| **3 — Strategic Partner** | Co-creation, co-brand | Rev share or flat fee | Joint offer, shared audience, shared risk |
| **4 — Certification Partner** | Licensed delivery / coach | Certification fee + backend % | Train them to implement; they promote to clients |

**Architect rule:** Start Tier 1–2 for Kindle/backend launches. Graduate to Tier 3–4 when offer and proof support it.

## The 7-Step Partnership Launch Method

```
ATTRACT → IDENTIFY → INITIATE → INVITE → EQUIP → EXECUTE → CELEBRATE
```

| Step | Job | Fail signal |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Attract** | Become promotable — results, brand, offer quality | Partners ignore you; "not a fit" |
| **2. Identify** | Map ideal partner profile (audience overlap, values, reach) | Wrong list size, wrong avatar |
| **3. Initiate** | Give before ask — value, intro, promotion of *their* work | Cold pitch with no relationship |
| **4. Invite** | Clear proposal: dates, commission, effort required, exclusivity | Vague "want to partner?" |
| **5. Equip** | Full partner pack — swipes, calendar, training, tracking | Partner has to write from scratch |
| **6. Execute** | Coordinated launch window + leaderboard + support | Partners mail random days |
| **7. Celebrate** | Share results, testimonial, plan next launch | One-and-done; partner never returns |

## Ideal Partner Profile (IPP)

Score partners before outreach:

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience overlap** | High | Do they serve growth operators, founders, agencies, industrial B2B — your avatars? |
| **Trust depth** | High | Will their list act on a recommendation? |
| **Non-competition** | High | Complementary, not identical offer? |
| **Promotion history** | Medium | Have they mailed for others successfully? |
| **Values alignment** | Medium | DR + substance, not hype-only? |
| **Reach** | Medium | List size matters less than **buyer quality** |
| **Responsiveness** | Low | Will they hit deadlines during launch week? |

**Minimum score to pursue:** 7/10 on overlap + trust + non-competition.

## Partnership Models (pick one per launch)

### Model A — Summit / Interview Series

You host. Partners are featured guests. They promote registration to their list.

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | "Free [X]-day summit for [avatar]" |
| **Partner ask** | 2–3 emails to list + optional social |
| **Partner win** | Authority positioning + affiliate on backend offer |
| **Your win** | List growth + goodwill + segmented leads |

**Burchard pattern:** 20–30 experts → each mails once → you own the registrant relationship.

### Model B — Co-Hosted Challenge

5–7 day live challenge. Partner co-brands; split promotion.

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Daily live** | Teaching + homework + community |
| **Partner role** | Co-host 1–2 days OR exclusive bonus for their list |
| **Close** | Cart at end — partner commission on sales |

### Model C — Affiliate Launch (JV Pack)

Classic coordinated cart open. See `EVENT-LAUNCH-ARCHITECTURE-METHODOLOGY.md` + `research/JV-PARTNER-PACK-TEMPLATE.md`.

### Model D — Certification Partner

Train practitioners on your framework (Marketing Machine™, Agent systems). They pay certification fee + promote implementation to clients.

| Requirement | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Curriculum** | Licensed modules + exams or project |
| **Brand guidelines** | How they represent Freeman Intelligence |
| **Backend** | Ongoing rev share on tools/templates they sell |

### Model E — Industrial Strategic Alliance

For FI-093 *Industrial Demand Machine* — logistics, 3PL, manufacturing associations, trade media.

| Partner type | Offer |
|---|---|
| Association / publication | Sponsored content + webinar + lead share |
| Complementary SaaS | Integration partner + co-webinar |
| Consultant network | White-label demand machine build |

## Partner Offer Design (make it irresistible to promote)

Partners promote when **their audience wins** and **they win**.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Commission** | 40–50% digital; 20–30% high-ticket services (adjust for fulfillment cost) |
| **Partner-exclusive bonus** | Not available on your main page — only via partner link |
| **Cookie / attribution window** | 30–90 days minimum for evergreen |
| **Leaderboard prizes** | Top 3 partners: cash, done-for-you, or VIP day |
| **Swipe completeness** | 7 emails + 10 social + 3 ads — **zero writing required** |
| **Partner training call** | 30 min: offer, angles, objections, links |
| **Real scarcity** | Cart close date true; partner can mail "last chance" with integrity |

## Partner Recruitment Sequence

### Phase 1 — Warm network (weeks 1–2)

1. List 50 potential partners (IPP score each)
2. Engage their content — genuine comments, shares, intros
3. Promote *their* best work to your audience (Initiate step)
4. Soft DM: "Love what you're doing with [X] — open to a collaboration idea?"

### Phase 2 — Invitation (week 3)

**Partner invitation email structure:**

```
1. Specific compliment (their work, not flattery)
2. Alignment statement (shared audience)
3. The opportunity (summit slot / JV / challenge)
4. What they get (commission, exposure, exclusive bonus)
5. What you need (dates, # of emails, deadline)
6. Low-friction next step (15-min call or reply YES)
```

### Phase 3 — Onboarding (week 4)

- Partner brief (1 page)
- Unique tracking link
- Swipe pack + calendar of when to mail
- Partner FAQ + compliance disclaimers
- Slack/Discord channel for launch week

### Phase 4 — Launch week

| Day | Partner action | Your support |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | Optional teaser post | Send asset reminder |
| **Cart open** | Email 1 (story) | Monitor clicks; fix broken links |
| **Mid-launch** | Email 2 (proof) | Share live stats (social proof for partners) |
| **Close** | Email 3 (urgency) | Leaderboard update |
| **Post-close** | Thank you + results share | Plan next collab |
