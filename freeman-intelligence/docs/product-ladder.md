# Freeman Intelligence — Product Ladder

Four products form the revenue ladder from first touch to recurring intelligence infrastructure.

---

## Product 1: Revenue Intelligence Audit

**Entry offer · Diagnostic gateway**

### Analyzes

- Revenue flows and leakage points
- Website and digital presence
- Sales funnel and pipeline health
- Customer journey friction
- Operations and handoff bottlenecks
- Data systems and reporting gaps
- Automation and AI readiness

### Output

**Revenue Opportunity Map** — prioritized opportunities with estimated impact bands and recommended sequence.

### Delivery

- Powered by the **Revenue Opportunity Diagnostic** (interactive tool)
- 45-minute executive briefing on findings
- Written brief (PDF) within 5 business days for qualified leads

### Price guidance

| Tier | Scope | Indicative |
|------|-------|------------|
| Self-serve diagnostic | Online tool + automated brief | Free (lead gen) |
| Guided audit | Diagnostic + 45-min briefing | $2,500–$5,000 |
| Full audit | On-site/virtual deep dive + custom map | $7,500–$15,000 |

---

## Product 2: Website Revenue Intelligence Audit

**Powered by WRIS**

### Analyzes

- Market positioning vs. competitors
- Customer voice and ICP fit
- Website copy, UX, and conversion paths
- Interactive and engagement opportunities
- SEO and local search presence
- Competitive digital landscape

### Output

**Website Revenue Blueprint** — section-by-section recommendations, copy direction, interactive elements, and conversion architecture.

### WRIS stack

```
WRIS Research → Blue Ocean Analysis → USP → EWC Copy → Website Blueprint
```

### Price guidance

$5,000–$12,000 depending on site complexity and competitive depth.

## Product 3: Revenue Systems Engineering

**Implementation · Core revenue engine**

### Delivers

- CRM configuration and revenue workflows
- Executive dashboards and KPI monitoring
- AI agents (proposal, inbound, QA, custom)
- Automation (email, data sync, reporting)
- Websites and web applications
- Integration between disconnected systems

### Output

Working revenue infrastructure with documented runbooks and handoff.

### Components from this monorepo

| Capability | Product |
|------------|---------|
| RFP / proposal automation | AI Proposals Agent™ |
| Plant inbound readiness | Freeman Intel |
| Software delivery agents | Developer / Architect / EM agents |
| SaaS factory patterns | saas-factory/ |

### Price guidance

Project-based: $25,000–$150,000+ depending on scope. Retainer option for ongoing engineering.

---

## Product 4: Intelligence Infrastructure

**Recurring revenue layer**

### Ongoing service

- Market monitoring (Metro Detroit industrial corridor)
- Competitive intelligence alerts
- Revenue reporting and pipeline analytics
- Executive briefings (monthly/quarterly)
- Opportunity detection from operational signals
- AI-generated intelligence summaries
- KPI monitoring and anomaly alerts

### Output

Monthly Intelligence Brief + live dashboard access.

### Price guidance

$3,000–$8,000/month depending on data sources and briefing frequency.
