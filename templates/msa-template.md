## 1. Services
HUNTER provides operational intelligence and systems integration services as defined in individual Statements of Work ("SOWs") executed under this Agreement.

## 2. Statements of Work
Each project is governed by a separate SOW specifying scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees. SOWs are incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

## 3. Fees & Payment
- 50% deposit due upon SOW execution
- 50% balance due upon delivery acceptance
- Net 7 days on all invoices
- Late payments accrue 1.5% monthly interest

## 4. Intellectual Property
Upon full payment, Client receives ownership of all custom deliverables. HUNTER retains ownership of pre-existing tools, frameworks, and methodologies.

## 5. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to protect confidential information disclosed during the engagement for a period of 3 years.

## 6. Warranty
HUNTER warrants deliverables will materially conform to SOW specifications for 30 days post-delivery. HUNTER will remedy defects at no additional cost.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
HUNTER's total liability shall not exceed fees paid under the applicable SOW.

## 8. Term & Termination
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Client pays for work completed through termination date.
